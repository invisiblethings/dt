This policy explains what personal information Pianoers.com collects, why, who sees it, and what choices you have. We try to collect as little as we can. We don't sell your data, and we don't run ads on this site.

The short version

  • We use analytics to see which pages are read. We use Google Analytics and the analytics built into our publishing software, Ghost.
  • If you sign up for our email list, sign in, or comment, we store your email address and what you submit.
  • If you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address and message.
  • Some links on this site are affiliate links. We can earn a commission if you buy through them, and the stores and networks use cookies to credit us. We never see your payment details.
  • YouTube videos on some pages, and a few other tools, load from third parties that can see your IP address.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at info@pianoers.com.

What we collect, and why

When you visit the site

Our web server records standard technical information about each request: your IP address, the page you asked for, the date and time, your browser and device type, and the page that sent you here. We use this to deliver the site, keep it secure, fix errors and spot abuse.

Analytics

We want to know which articles help people and which don't. We use two tools:

  • Ghost analytics. This is built into our publishing software and runs on our own server. It records page views with a random identifier stored in your browser's local storage. It does not use cookies and doesn't follow you to other websites.
  • Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager. It sets cookies (_ga and _ga_4J8LKV62M8) and collects pseudonymous data such as pages viewed, time on site, device and browser, the site you came from, and your approximate location worked out from your IP address. Google processes this data for us.

Our Google tag setup also includes Google's conversion measurement. It can set a cookie called _gcl_au and store a value in local storage, and it contacts Google advertising servers (for example doubleclick.net). We use it to measure how visits lead to clicks on affiliate links. We don't show third-party ads on this site.

Email list, sign-in and comments

If you subscribe, sign in or comment, we collect your email address, and your name if you give it. We store this in Ghost on our own server. We use it to send you the emails you asked for (a confirmation link, our newsletter, sign-in links) and, for comments, to show your name next to your comment, which is public. Newsletters and sign-in emails are sent through an email delivery service. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing us.

Contact form

When you send a message through our contact page, your name, email address and message go to our form provider, Formcarry (formcarry.com), which passes them to us by email. We use them only to reply to you. We keep a message for as long as we need to deal with it and for a reasonable time afterwards in case you write again.

Content from other websites

  • YouTube. Some articles embed YouTube videos. When a page with a video loads, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and can set cookies. See our Cookie Policy for the details.
  • Ghost scripts from jsDelivr. The sign-in and comment tools load from the jsDelivr network (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which receives your IP address when they load.
  • Images. A few images load from Unsplash's servers (images.unsplash.com).

Things your browser remembers for the site

Some site features save small settings in your own browser and never send them to us: your sound on/off choice for the piano, your reading history so we can show "Continue reading", your best score in the ear-training game, and whether you closed the "recommended piano" bar. Clear your browser's site data to remove them.

Affiliate links

Some links on Pianoers.com are affiliate links, to Amazon, online piano courses and other sellers. Some of them go through short links on our own site, such as pianoers.com/PFA, before they reach the seller. If you click one and buy, the seller or its affiliate network (for example Amazon Associates, ClickBank or Impact) may pay us a commission. To do that they set their own cookies when you click, which they use under their own privacy policies. They tell us about commissions earned. They don't tell us who you are or what you bought.

Pianoers.com is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. Our recommendations are not for sale. See our disclosure on any post marked as containing affiliate links.
