This policy explains how Pianoers.com uses cookies and similar technologies, which ones you may meet on this site, and how to control them. It goes with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we use personal information more generally.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. "Local storage" works in a similar way: it lets a website save a small piece of data in your browser. We call both of these "cookies" below unless the difference matters. Cookies from the site you're visiting are first-party cookies. Cookies from another company, such as YouTube or Google, are third-party cookies.

How to control cookies

We don't currently show a cookie banner, so the analytics, conversion-measurement and (on pages with videos) YouTube cookies above load when you visit. You can block or delete them at any time, and the site works without them:

  • Browser settings. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies and site data. Look for "Cookies and site data" or "Privacy" in the settings. Blocking third-party cookies stops most of the Google and YouTube cookies.
  • Google Analytics. Install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Ad and tracking settings. Use Google's My Ad Center to control personalised ads, or a browser extension that blocks trackers.
  • Local storage. Clear "site data" for pianoers.com in your browser to remove the features' saved settings.

If you block the features' own storage, things like "Continue reading" and your game score will not be remembered, but you can still read everything.
