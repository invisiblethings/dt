## 3. Rules
1. Put links **inside the body text**, in the sentence where the topic comes up.
2. Use **3 to 8 new links per post**. Link each page **once** per post, at the first natural mention.
3. **Don't link** headings, buttons, image captions or the quick-answer box.
4. Keep anchors short and descriptive. Never use "here" or "click here".
5. For each new post: add it to the anchor bank, and link it from its hub on the day it goes live.
6. In Ghost: select the words, press **Ctrl/Cmd + K**, and paste the full URL.
7. Where a sentence is suggested, put it at the end of the paragraph that's closest in topic. Edit the wording so it sounds like you.
