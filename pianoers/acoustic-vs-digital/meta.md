## How to publish
1. **Images:** upload the 8 new inline images in October 2026 (everything in `images/` except the featured and social images). If you upload in November, replace `2026/10` with `2026/11` in `article.html`. The side-by-side photo is your existing image from 2025/11, so it needs no upload.
2. **Article:** replace the post body with one HTML card containing `article.html`. The quiz's style and script are at the end of that card.
3. **Fields:** set the fields above, update the post, and request indexing.

## What changed
- **Length:** 826 words became about 3,500.
- **Images:** 8 new diagrams and charts, plus your existing photo.
- **Quiz:** a 5-question "acoustic or digital" quiz. All 108 answer combinations were tested, and it never recommends an acoustic for a budget under $1,000 or for night-time practice.
- **Fixed claims from the old version:**
  - "$150–$250 every 6 months / $300–$600 a year". Now the Piano Technicians Guild schedule, at about $100–$200 a visit.
  - "indistinguishable in blind tests" and "95% real". Now plain descriptions of what does and doesn't transfer.
  - Upright depth "2 feet+". Now 23–26 in.
- **Sources linked:** the Piano Technicians Guild (tuning and humidity) and Steinway (care).
- **Internal links:** 17, to the digital-piano and beginner-piano guides, the P-145 review, the care and humidity guides, how the piano works, the apps guide and the teach-yourself guide.
