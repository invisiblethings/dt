    Quick answer

    The best beginner keyboard piano for most people is the Yamaha P-145BT.

    It costs about $500, has fully weighted keys, a clean piano tone and Bluetooth audio. On a tight budget, get the Alesis Recital Pro (about $400). If key feel matters most, the Roland FP-10 has the same keys as Roland's pricier FP-30X. For the best sound around $500, pick the Kawai ES60.
