# Hover Chords privacy policy

Hover Chords is a Chrome extension made by Pianoers.com.

**What it collects:** nothing. Hover Chords does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data or browsing data.

**What it does on the pages you visit:** it reads the text of the page in your browser to find chord names, then adds the chord diagrams. This happens entirely on your computer. No page content ever leaves your browser.

**What it saves:** your settings (whether it's on for a website, how far you've transposed, and whether sound is on), saved with Chrome's own storage on your computer. You can clear them at any time by removing the extension.

**Third parties:** none. There are no analytics, ads, trackers or outside servers. The only outside link is the "Pianoers.com" link, which opens our website if you click it.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent through https://pianoers.com/contact/
