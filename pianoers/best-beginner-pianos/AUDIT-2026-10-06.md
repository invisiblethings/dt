## Findings, most severe first

### High

1. **The image at the top pushes the article down and makes the page jump (seo-technical, seo-images, seo-agentic).**
   - The featured image is portrait (1024×1536), with no `width`/`height` and no `fetchpriority`.
   - On desktop it renders at 720×1080. The intro starts 2.1 screens down (1.2 on a phone), and the phone's first screen has no article text.
   - Lab layout shift on desktop was **0.272**, above Google's "poor" line of 0.25. It's caused by the image loading in and pushing the caption and content down.
   - It is also the page's LCP element.
   - Fix: use a landscape image, or give the theme's image tag dimensions and `fetchpriority="high"`.
2. **Overlap with `/best-digital-piano/` (seo-cluster, seo-sxo).**
   - The broader page has its own H2, "Best Digital Pianos for Beginners (Under $500)".
   - That page shows up for "best digital piano for beginners" and "best beginner digital piano 2026". This article doesn't appear for either.
   - The two searches share about one top-10 result with "best beginner keyboard piano", so Google treats this article as the "keyboard" page and the other one as the "digital piano for beginners" page.
   - Fix: make `/best-digital-piano/` the main page and this article its beginner sub-page.
     - Shorten the other page's beginner section to a summary that links here with the anchor "best digital pianos for beginners".
     - Work "digital piano" into this article's title and H1, e.g. "Beginner Keyboard & Digital Pianos".
3. **The article claims testing but doesn't show it (seo-content, seo-sxo, seo-content-brief).**
   - 3 of the top 5 competitors have a "why trust us / how we tested" section. `/best-digital-piano/` already has "How I Tested These Pianos". This article has none.
   - Fix: add the method, real photos, and Katarina's credentials in the article itself (fix kit step 5).
4. **Affiliate links and disclosure:** no `rel="sponsored"`, and the disclosure comes after the last word of the article. Fix kit steps 1–2.
5. **Contradictions:** FP-10/FP-30X key action, and three different budget ranges. Fix kit steps 3–4.

### Medium

6. **Missing topic: portable and 61-key keyboards (seo-content-brief, seo-sxo).**
   - All five competitors cover cheaper portable keyboards (Casio CT-S1, Yamaha NP-15, Roland GO:KEYS, Korg Liano) for kids, casual players or budgets under $350. This article tells those readers to go elsewhere.
   - Fix: a short "If you only want a keyboard to try (or for a young child)" section with 2–3 picks. Katarina's teaching experience covers this credibly, and it can link to the Loog review.
7. **`www.pianoers.com` is dead (seo-technical).** Public DNS returns 192.0.2.1 and `100::`, both placeholder addresses that can't be reached. Anyone who types `www.`, and any backlink using it, gets an error. Fix: point `www` at the server and 301-redirect it to the bare domain.
8. **Schema gaps (seo-schema):**
   - No ItemList (in the fix kit).
   - No BreadcrumbList.
   - The author in the Article schema has no job title or description.
   - Two competitors use Product schema.
   - FAQPage is valid but no longer produces rich results (retired May 2026). Keep it. One answer's wording in the schema differs slightly from the visible text.
9. **AI-search citability (seo-geo):**
   - No answer-first summary at the top; the quick verdict (TL;DR) is at the very bottom.
   - Each product section opens with a quote, not a one-line verdict.
   - Fix: add a 2–3 sentence "Quick answer" under the intro naming the top pick and why. Start each product section with a one-sentence verdict that stands on its own.
10. **Conversion (seo-flow):**
    - All seven calls to action are the same "Current Price on Amazon" button.
    - The bottom recap has no links.
    - No price-checked date.
    - No second retailer, even though the article mentions Sweetwater and Guitar Center.
    - Fix: link each line of the bottom recap to its section and button, and add the price-checked date.
11. **Freshness:** only "Mar 29, 2026" is shown on the page. The sitemap's last-modified date is 14 April 2026. No visible "updated" date.
12. **WebP not served (seo-images):** about 31–37% smaller in WebP (hero 111 KB → 70 KB; product images 61 KB → 42 KB). This is a theme change.
13. **Title phrase barely used:** "beginner keyboard piano(s)" appears once in the body.

### Low

14. **Security headers:** only HSTS is set. CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options and Referrer-Policy are missing (server config).
15. **Accessibility for agents and screen readers:** the mobile menu button (`gh-burger`) has no accessible name. Theme fix: add `aria-label="Menu"`.
16. **`llms.txt` is out of date:** it lists this article as "5 Best…" (the article has 7) and leaves out `/best-digital-piano/`. Google ignores this file, so it's cosmetic.
17. **Mobile table:** the comparison table is wider than the content column, so readers swipe sideways.
18. **Other image issues:**
    - No image has `decoding="async"`.
    - The theme's logo, author photo and "Read next" thumbnails have no dimensions.
    - The featured image's alt text repeats the H1, and its caption says "(TESTED)".
19. **Wording:** "game-changer" is the only stock AI phrase. No hidden Unicode characters found.

## What's already good

- Server-rendered, with all article text in the raw HTML.
- Self-pointing canonical, no `noindex`, page is in the sitemap.
- Redirects work: `http` → `https`, and missing trailing slash or uppercase URL → the canonical URL.
- Unknown URLs return a real 404.
- First byte in about 0.2 s, HTML is 54 KB, scripts are deferred, images are cached for a year.
- robots.txt allows all crawlers, including OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Googlebot.
- Easy to read: Flesch 66 (about grade 7), about 11 words per sentence, about 3 sentences per paragraph.
- 2,839 words, one H1, a clean H2 order, and a comparison table near the top.
- The page appears in roughly the top 4 for "best beginner keyboard piano", and its page type matches the results (product roundups).

## Top five actions

1. Add `rel="sponsored"` and move the disclosure up (fix kit 1–2).
2. Fix the two contradictions and add "How I tested" plus credentials (fix kit 3–5).
3. Replace the portrait featured image or add dimensions to it (#1).
4. Re-scope against `/best-digital-piano/` and work "digital piano" into the title and H1 (#2).
5. Add a "Quick answer" at the top and the portable-keyboard section (#6, #9).
