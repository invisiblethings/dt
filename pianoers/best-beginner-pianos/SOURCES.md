# Fact check and sources: best-beginner-pianos

Checked on 6 October 2026. Every product fact in `article-revised.html` is listed below with where it was checked.

**How each fact was checked:**
- **Opened**: I read the page or PDF myself.
- **Search snippet**: the site blocked automated access (Sweetwater, Casio.com, some Guitar Center pages), so the value comes from the search engine's summary of that page. Treat these as good but not certain, and spot-check them in a browser.

**Prices change.** Re-check them before publishing and put the date in the article's "Prices checked" line.

## What a fact check can't confirm

These are Katarina's own judgements or experience. They can't be sourced, and they're fine to publish as opinion if they're true for her:

- **Feel and sound:** how each piano feels or sounds ("feels right", "sounds surprisingly warm", "lighter than Roland's PHA-4", "hammer action doesn't feel as refined").
- **Experience:** "My go-to recommendation for over a year" and "I've tested tons of beginner-friendly digital pianos".
- **Rankings and "best for" labels:** these are editorial choices.
- **The "How I Tested" section:** it must describe what she actually did, which is why it's left as placeholders.
