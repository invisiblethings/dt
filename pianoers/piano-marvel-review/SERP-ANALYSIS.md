## 1. What the searcher wants

**Intent: commercial investigation.** The person has heard of Piano Marvel and is deciding whether to try it or pay for it. The questions behind the search, in the order they come up:

1. What is it, and is it any good? Give me a verdict fast.
2. What does it cost, and is the free plan enough?
3. Will it work for me: my level, my goal, and my piano or keyboard (MIDI or microphone)?
4. What do real people say? This is why the Reddit thread ranks.
5. How does it compare with Simply Piano, Flowkey, Playground Sessions and others?
6. How do I start?

**What Google is rewarding:** two blogs and one Reddit thread. When Reddit ranks for a "review" query, Google is short of pages with **first-hand experience** and real-user opinion. The two blogs are old (2021, and 2022 with a 2025 date stamp), and neither has an author bio or test details. So the bar is low, and **experience plus current facts** is how to clear it.

## 3. Gaps we can own

1. **Current, dated prices**: Free $0, Premium $17.99 a month, Annual $129.99 ($10.84 a month), checked Oct 2026. Both blogs show 2021 prices.
2. **A free versus Premium table.** Nobody shows what the free plan actually gives you (150+ songs, 200+ exercises, 25 videos).
3. **SASR explained properly**, with the real mechanics:
   - 18 levels split into 90 sublevels, from 1a to 18e,
   - 20 seconds to look over each piece,
   - 80% to move up a level, and three strikes end the test,
   - a top score of 1,900.
   This is Piano Marvel's unique feature, and no top result explains it.
4. **Setup guide:** which cable, MIDI versus the newer microphone mode, which pianos work. This is the biggest practical question.
5. **Who should skip it.** The competitors are sponsored or push affiliates. Saying plainly who it doesn't suit, like song-first casual learners, builds trust and conversions.
6. **Comparison table** against the four alternatives readers actually consider, linked to our own reviews.
7. **What users say**: App Store (4.7 from about 5,500 ratings) and Reddit themes, including the complaints (scoring of grace notes, slow support, clunky uploads on iPad).
8. **A first-week plan**, so the reader can act straight away (information gain plus conversion).
9. **FAQ** answering the long-tail questions:
   - Is it free? Does it work with an acoustic piano? On iPad, Android or Chromebook?
   - How do I cancel?
   - What is a good SASR score?
10. **Author and method box** (E-E-A-T): who tested it, on what piano, and for how long.
