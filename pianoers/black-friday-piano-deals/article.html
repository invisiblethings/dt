5 checks before you click "buy"

  1. Exact model. The Yamaha P-145 and P-145BT look identical; only the BT has Bluetooth. The original Alesis Recital Pro and the MKII share photos; only the MKII has a pedal in the box.
  2. Who sells it. Look for "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or the brand's store.
  3. Price history. A tracker like camelcamelcamel or Keepa shows whether the "deal" is really lower than usual.
  4. Bundles. A good piano-style pedal and sturdy stand are worth it. Toy footswitches and earbuds aren't.
  5. Returns. Amazon usually extends holiday returns into January. Keep the box until you're sure.

Black Friday "deals" to skip

  • No-name "88-key weighted" pianos under $250. Springy keys and a thin sound. You'll want to replace it within months.
  • 61-key keyboards sold as "pianos". Fine for a curious child, not for learning piano properly. My piano basics guide explains why.
  • "Lessons included" bundles. Usually a short trial of an app you could get free anyway.
