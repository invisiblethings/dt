How to tell if a Black Friday piano deal is real

  1. Check the price history. Paste the Amazon link into a price tracker such as camelcamelcamel or Keepa. If the "deal" price is the same as last month's price, it isn't a deal.
  2. Check the exact model. Listings mix up old and new versions. The Yamaha P-145 and P-145BT look identical; only the BT has Bluetooth audio. The original Alesis Recital Pro and the Recital Pro MKII share photos; only the MKII has a pedal in the box.
  3. Check who sells it. Look for "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or the brand's own store. Odd prices from unknown third-party sellers are where returns and warranties get messy.
  4. Check the bundle. A "bundle" with a flimsy X-stand, a toy footswitch and earbuds isn't worth $80 more. A good piano-style pedal and a sturdy stand are worth paying for; the rest usually isn't.
  5. Check the return window. Amazon usually extends returns on holiday purchases into January. Keep the box until you're sure: key feel is personal.

Deals to skip

  • No-name "88-key weighted" pianos under $250. The keys are usually springy or semi-weighted, and the piano sound is thin. You'll want to replace it within months.
  • 61-key keyboards sold as "pianos". Fine for a curious child, but not for learning piano properly. See my piano basics guide for why 88 weighted keys matter.
  • "Lessons included" bundles. Most include a short trial of an app you could get free anyway.

Black Friday piano deals FAQ

When do Black Friday piano deals end?

Most run through Cyber Monday, November 30, 2026. Some stores keep prices low into early December, but the best stock and prices are usually gone by then.

Is Black Friday a good time to buy a digital piano?

Yes. It's one of the few times a year when popular beginner pianos like the Yamaha P-145BT and Roland FP-10 drop below their usual price. Just compare the deal with the price history, because some "was" prices are inflated.

What's the best Black Friday digital piano for a beginner?

The Yamaha P-145BT under $450 for most beginners, the Roland FP-10 under $450 if key feel matters most, and the Alesis Recital Pro MKII under $320 on a tight budget.

What is a good Black Friday price for the Yamaha P-145BT?

Anything under $450 is a good price. Yamaha's list price is $499.99.

What is a good Black Friday price for the Roland FP-10?

Anything under $450 is a good price for the FP-10.

Should I buy a piano bundle on Black Friday?

Only if the bundle includes things you'd buy anyway, like a piano-style sustain pedal and a sturdy stand. Bundles with a basic footswitch, a flimsy stand and earbuds are rarely worth the extra money.

Can I return a digital piano bought on Black Friday?

Usually, yes. Amazon normally extends its return window for holiday purchases into January. Check the return date on your order, and keep the box until you've played the piano for a few weeks.
