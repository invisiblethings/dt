1. **Images:** upload the 8 inline images in October 2026, so they live under `/content/images/2026/10/`. The verdict card reuses your existing `2026/03/Roland-FP10-Reviewed.jpg`, which needs no upload.
2. **Article:** paste `article.html` into one HTML card. The verdict card (`pz-pick`) turns on the sticky "Check price" bar, and its `data-name` keeps the bar label short.
3. **Amazon link:** both buttons use your existing FP-10 link, `https://amzn.to/3NSYD8n` (the same one as in /best-beginner-pianos/). Make sure it still points to the FP-10.

## Real photos to add (your own photos help most)
Your own photos are the strongest proof of a hands-on review for Google and for readers. If you can, take these and replace or add to the graphics:
1. The FP-10 on its stand in your room, wide shot (good for the top, or as the featured photo).
2. Close-up of the keys at an angle, showing the matte ivory-feel white keys.
3. The control buttons on the left of the panel.
4. The back connections: USB, headphone jack and pedal.
5. The included pedal switch next to a DP-10, to illustrate the upgrade.
6. Headphones plugged in, showing the 3.5 mm jack (with an adapter if you use one).
7. The Roland Piano App on a phone, connected over Bluetooth.

Upload them and add them as normal image cards in Ghost. Use descriptive file names such as `roland-fp-10-keys-close-up.jpg` and alt text such as "Close-up of the Roland FP-10's ivory-feel keys". Don't copy photos from Roland's or retailers' websites without permission.
