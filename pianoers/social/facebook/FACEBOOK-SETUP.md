## 1. Create the Page (about 15 minutes)

You need a personal Facebook account to create a Page. It stays private: visitors see only the Page, never your profile.

1. On a computer, go to **facebook.com/pages/create**. Or use the menu → **Pages** → **Create new Page**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Page name:** `Pianoers`
   - **Category:** type and choose **Education Website**. If it isn't offered, choose **Website**. You can add up to 3 categories: also add **Music** and **Education**. Don't pick a store category, since Pianoers doesn't sell instruments.
   - **Bio** (101 characters max): see section 2.
3. Click **Create Page**. Then, as Facebook asks:
   - **Website:** `https://pianoers.com`
   - **Email:** the address you use on the site's contact page. Or skip it and rely on Messenger.
   - **Location, hours, phone:** leave these empty. Pianoers is an online publication.
4. Upload the **profile picture**, then the **cover photo**. When asked, drag the cover so its centre stays in view.
5. Skip "Connect WhatsApp" and "Invite friends" for now. Invite friends after the first 5 posts are up, so the Page doesn't look empty.

### Username (your Page's web address)
Go to **Page settings → Page setup → Username** (or the **Edit** button under the Page name). Try these in order:
1. `pianoers`
2. `pianoerscom`
3. `pianoers.official`

Your Page address becomes `facebook.com/<username>`.

### Action button
Click **+ Add action button** (or **Edit action button**) → **Learn more** → **Visit website** → `https://pianoers.com`.

Later, once you want sign-ups, change it to **Sign up** → `https://pianoers.com/#/portal/signup`.

## 3. Settings to change on day one

- **Messaging → Instant reply: On.** Use this text:
  > Thanks for writing to Pianoers! 🎹 We usually reply within a day or two. In the meantime, our guides are at pianoers.com.
- **Messaging → Frequently asked questions:** add these 3:
  - *Which digital piano should I buy?* → link to `https://pianoers.com/best-digital-piano/`
  - *What's the best way to learn piano online?* → link to `https://pianoers.com/best-piano-lessons-online/`
  - *Which piano is best for a beginner?* → link to `https://pianoers.com/best-beginner-pianos/`
- **Turn on two-factor authentication** on your personal account (Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security). Page hijacking is common.
- **Add a second admin** you trust (Page settings → Page access), so you can't be locked out.
- Install **Meta Business Suite** (app or business.facebook.com) to schedule posts in advance.

## 4. Connect the Page to your website

1. **Ghost Admin → Settings → General → Social accounts → Facebook:** paste your Page URL (`https://www.facebook.com/<username>`) and save.
   - Ghost then adds the Page to its share metadata (`article:publisher`), which tells Google and Facebook that the Page and the site belong together.
   - **Theme 1.5.4** (the attached `pianoers.zip`) shows a "Follow Pianoers.com on Facebook" link in the footer automatically once this field is filled.
2. **Upload theme 1.5.4.** It also includes the preferred-source button (1.5.3) and the feature-image fix (1.5.2).
3. **Check your share previews.** Paste a few article URLs into the [Sharing Debugger](https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/) and click "Scrape again". It shows the image and title Facebook will use, and refreshes old cached previews.

## 6. Growing the Page: what works and what to avoid

**Do**
- **Answer every comment** in the first hour after posting. Replies are what makes Facebook show a post to more people.
- **Post short videos (Reels)** when you can: 15–40 seconds of you playing a chord trick or showing a practice tip. Even simple phone videos work.
- **Share each new article** the day it's published, with one personal sentence on why you wrote it.
- **Join 2–3 piano groups** (adult beginners, piano teachers) and help people there. Link to your guides only when they truly answer someone's question.

**Avoid**
- **"Like if… / Share if…" posts.** Facebook demotes engagement bait.
- **Buying likes or followers.** Fake accounts never engage, so your real reach drops.
- **Posting only links.** Mix in tips, questions and your own photos, roughly 1 link post in every 2–3.
