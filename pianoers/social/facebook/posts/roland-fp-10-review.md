## Main post
Roland FP-10 review: does it really have the best key feel under $500? 🎹

For most learners, yes. The FP-10 is Roland's cheapest 88-key digital piano, but it uses the same PHA-4 Standard keys as the pricier FP-30X. My score: 4.3 out of 5.

What it does brilliantly:
✅ PHA-4 Standard keys with escapement and ivory-feel key tops, the most piano-like action at this price
✅ Roland's SuperNATURAL Piano sound, expressive at every volume
✅ 96-note polyphony, more than the Yamaha P-145's 64
✅ Bluetooth MIDI for learning apps like the Roland Piano App
✅ Twin Piano mode for lessons, side by side
✅ $499.99 list price, often less on sale

Where it cuts corners:
➖ Small 6 W + 6 W speakers
➖ No Bluetooth audio, so you can't stream music through it
➖ Only 15 sounds on the panel (36 with the app), and a basic pedal switch in the box

FP-10 or Yamaha P-145BT? Pick the FP-10 for heavier, more realistic keys and wireless MIDI. Pick the P-145BT for lighter, quieter keys and Bluetooth audio. The full side-by-side is in my review 👇

Buying tip: swap the basic pedal switch that comes in the box for a real piano-style sustain pedal, such as Roland's DP-10. It feels like an acoustic pedal and supports half-pedalling. It's the cheapest upgrade that makes the biggest difference.
