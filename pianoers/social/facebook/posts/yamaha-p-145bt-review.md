## Main post
Yamaha P-145BT review: is it still the best digital piano for beginners in 2026? 🎹

Short answer: for most beginners, yes. It's still my #1 pick under $500.

Here's why I keep recommending it:
✅ 88 fully weighted GHC keys that feel close to a real piano
✅ Yamaha's CFIIIS concert grand sound
✅ Bluetooth audio: play along with Spotify or YouTube through the piano's speakers
✅ Only 24.5 lbs (11.1 kg), so it fits a desk or a small apartment
✅ $499.99 list price, and often on sale

What it doesn't do as well:
➖ The 7 W + 7 W speakers are fine for a bedroom, but you'll want headphones in a bigger room
➖ 64-note polyphony, which is plenty for beginners but limiting years later

Quick tip if you're buying: the P-145BT's Bluetooth is for audio (streaming music). To connect learning apps, use a USB cable, or Yamaha's UD-BT01 adapter for wireless MIDI.
