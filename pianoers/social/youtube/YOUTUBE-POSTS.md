# YouTube posts for @Pianoers

Six image posts (1080 × 1080, YouTube's recommended square size, in `assets/`), plus polls and quizzes that use the post box's own **Text poll** and **Quiz** buttons.

**How to post an image:** click **Image**, upload the file, paste the text, then **Post** (or use the arrow next to it to **Schedule**).

## Image posts

### 1. Name this chord: `yt-quiz-name-this-chord.jpg`
> 🎹 Quick quiz! Three white keys, one famous chord. Can you name it?
>
> Drop your answer in the comments. I'll pin the answer tomorrow. 👇

**Pin this comment the next day:** *Answer: F major (F–A–C). Count it: F up 4 keys to A, then 3 more to C, the major chord rule!*

### 2. Which key is C?: `yt-quiz-find-the-c.jpg`
> Beginners, this one's for you! 🙋 Which numbered key is C: 1, 2, 3 or 4?
>
> Hint: look at the black keys.

**Pinned answer:** *It's 1! C is always the white key just left of the group of 2 black keys. Find that pattern anywhere on the keyboard and you've found a C.*

### 3. Name this note: `yt-quiz-name-this-note.jpg`
> Sight-reading check 📝 Treble clef, second line from the bottom. What's the note?

**Pinned answer:** *G! The treble clef is also called the G clef, because its curl wraps around the G line. The lines from the bottom are E G B D F: "Every Good Boy Does Fine".*

### 4. Four chords: `yt-tip-four-chords.jpg`
> 4 chords, hundreds of songs 🎶
>
> C – G – Am – F
>
> Play each one for 4 beats and loop it. You'll start hearing songs you know. It's the pattern musicians call I–V–vi–IV.
>
> Which song did you hear first? 👇

### 5. Major scale formula: `yt-tip-major-scale.jpg`
> Every major scale uses the same pattern: W W H W W W H.
>
> W = skip one key (white or black). H = the very next key.
>
> Start on C and you'll play only white keys. Start on G and you'll need one black key (F♯). Try a new starting note today!

### 6. 15-minute practice routine: `yt-tip-15-minute-practice.jpg`
> No time to practise? You have 15 minutes. ⏰
>
> 2 min warm-up · 5 min the hard part, slowly · 5 min review · 3 min just for fun
>
> Try it every day this week, and tell me on Sunday how it went! 💪

## Quizzes (click **Quiz**, then mark the correct answer)

**Quiz 1**
> How many keys does a full-size piano have?
- 61
- 76
- **88** ✅ *(explanation: 52 white keys and 36 black keys)*
- 100

**Quiz 2**
> In 4/4 time, how many beats does a half note get?
- 1
- **2** ✅ *(explanation: whole note = 4, half = 2, quarter = 1, eighth = ½)*
- 3
- 4

**Quiz 3**
> Which notes make a C major chord?
- C – D – E
- **C – E – G** ✅ *(explanation: root, then up 4 keys, then up 3)*
- C – F – A
- C – E♭ – G

**Quiz 4**
> In piano music, which finger is number 1?
- **Thumb** ✅ *(explanation: 1 thumb, 2 index, 3 middle, 4 ring, 5 pinky, the same in both hands)*
- Index finger
- Pinky
- It depends on the hand

**Quiz 5**
> What's another name for C♯?
- B♭
- **D♭** ✅ *(explanation: every black key has two names. C♯ is one half step above C, the same key as D♭)*
- D♯
- C♭

## Tips
- **Post 2–3 times a week.** Mix an image post, a poll and a quiz.
- **Reply to comments, and pin the answer** to quiz images the next day. It brings people back to the post.
- **Polls and quizzes are the easiest wins:** one tap to answer, and YouTube shows posts with lots of answers to more viewers.
- **Use Poll 2's results** to choose your next video. Then tell people you did ("You voted, here it is!").
