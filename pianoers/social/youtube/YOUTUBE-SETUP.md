The logo and text sit inside the safe area, so they show on phones, desktop and TV. The keyboard at the bottom only appears on TVs.

## Channel description (paste in YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info → Description)

> Honest piano reviews, easy lessons and practice tips from a piano teacher who has taught since 2001. 🎹
>
> Pianoers helps beginners and adults returning to the piano choose the right instrument, learn faster and enjoy every practice session.
>
> On this channel you'll find:
> • Digital piano reviews and comparisons (Yamaha, Roland, Kawai, Casio)
> • Piano app and online course reviews
> • Easy piano lessons: chords, scales, reading music and rhythm
> • Practice routines and tips that actually stick
> • Buying guides for every budget
>
> No brand pays to be featured. If a piano or app isn't worth your money, I'll tell you.
>
> Subscribe for new videos, and ask me anything in the comments. I reply to as many as I can.
>
> 📖 Written guides, reviews and free tools: https://pianoers.com

About 760 characters (the limit is 1,000). The first two lines are what YouTube shows in search and on the channel header, so they carry the main keywords: piano reviews, piano lessons, practice tips, piano teacher.

## Other settings in Customization → Basic info
- **Name:** `Pianoers`
- **Handle:** `@pianoers` (fallbacks: `@pianoerscom`, `@pianoersofficial`)
- **Links:**
  - "Pianoers.com" → `https://pianoers.com`
  - "Facebook" → your Facebook Page URL, once it exists
- **Contact email:** the address from the site's contact page (optional)

## Branding (Customization → Branding)
- **Picture:** `pianoers-youtube-profile-picture.png`
- **Banner image:** `pianoers-youtube-banner.jpg`. Check the device preview before saving: the logo and headline should show on all three.
- **Video watermark** (optional): the profile picture, shown "at the end of the video", links viewers to subscribe.

## Connect the channel to the website
In Ghost Admin → Settings → General → Social accounts, Ghost 6 has a YouTube field. Paste the channel URL (`https://www.youtube.com/@pianoers`). When you embed your own videos in articles, you'll be adding real hands-on content, which is what the reviews need most.
