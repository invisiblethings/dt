## How to publish
1. Upload the 12 inline images in `images/` (everything except the featured and social images) in **October 2026**, so Ghost stores them under `/content/images/2026/10/`. If you upload in November, find and replace `2026/10` with `2026/11` in `article.html` first.
2. Delete the old post body, add one **HTML card**, and paste all of `article.html`.
3. Set the fields above, then update the post. Ghost keeps the URL.
4. Check: the table of contents builds itself from the H2s; the "Try it now" chord and the "Name that note" game play sound; images load on mobile.
5. In Search Console, inspect the URL and click **Request indexing**.
