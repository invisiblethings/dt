Quick answer

The best online piano lessons for most adult beginners: Pianoforall

It costs $49 once, with lifetime access and a 60-day money-back guarantee, and it starts you on chords, so real songs come early. The catch: nothing listens to you play. If you want an app that hears every note, try Skoove ($149.99 a year) or Simply Piano. For sight-reading and exams, pick Piano Marvel (free tier, or $129.99 a year). For help from real teachers, Pianote ($279 a year as part of Musora). For a huge song list, Flowkey. Not sure? Take the 30-second quiz.
