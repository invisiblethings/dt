## Things I could not verify
- Simply Piano: current monthly, annual and trial length (prices are shown only in the app or checkout; sources conflict between $119.99 and $169.90 a year).
- Playground Sessions: any price, the MIDI requirement, platforms, song counts (login wall). Only the "co-created by Quincy Jones" wording was read on the vendor site.
- Pianoforall: video lesson count ("over 300"), the number of books (testimonials say 10; the listing shows 9 named sections), and the order of the books. SAVE20 code.
- Piano Marvel: whether feedback uses MIDI or microphone.
- Skoove: feedback method and trial length (pricing page renders only by JavaScript; help article says 14-day trial through a link).
- Musora: whether you can send a recording for teacher review; how Pianote-specific content is organised inside Musora.
- Piano by Pictures: feedback, devices, Play by Ear module.
- Third-party reviews of Pianoforall: only Top Ten Reviews (2021) was opened. "Dated look" is not explicitly echoed; the video/audio quality comment is the closest.
