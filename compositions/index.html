Sheet music & scores

Few of Tom’s scores are widely published. “Delphinium Rag” appears in Ragtime Wizardry, Vol. 1 (Rivermont, BSW-2228), and “Green Clover,” a collaboration with Neil Blaze, Eric Marchese and Galen Wilkes, appears in Galen Wilkes’s Piano Rhythm folio at galenwilkes.com. For other requests, please get in touch.

Tom Brier

Ragtime pianist and composer from Oakdale, California. More than 200 piano compositions, including “Razor Blades.”
