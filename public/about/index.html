The company

Who we are

AXE Contracting Group is a general contractor based on Staten Island, working across the island and in Brooklyn and Manhattan. We take on residential and commercial work of almost every size: roofs and roof leaks, interior and exterior painting, kitchens and bathrooms, floors, basements, additions, pavers, decks and siding, and storefront renovations.

Most of our clients find us the way people on Staten Island find any contractor: a neighbor, a coworker, a review. So we run the business to keep earning those referrals. Angel (Engjel to some clients) answers the phone, does the walkthroughs, writes the quotes and stays in touch through the job. Reviewers mention it again and again: “responsive,” “easy to communicate with,” “always calling to make sure that everything is fine.”

We're a general contractor, not a one-trade shop. Our crew does the carpentry, roofing, painting, tile, flooring and masonry. For work that NYC requires licensed trades to perform, we bring in licensed electricians and plumbers we work with regularly, and they've vouched for us too.

How we work

General notes

  1. One contractor, every trade.

    Roofing, carpentry, tile, paint and pavers by our crew. Licensed electricians and plumbers coordinated under the same contract and schedule.

  2. We answer the phone.

    “When I first called Angel answered to me and since then he has always been calling to make sure that everything is fine.” (Elvi J.)

  3. Paperwork handled.

  4. Fair, written pricing.

  5. Clean, serious crews.

    “Very serious people, clean job.” (Donny S.) Job sites are protected while we work and swept when we leave.
