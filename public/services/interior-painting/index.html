Interior Painting

Scope of work

Interior painting is what most of our reviews talk about. People mention how fast the quote came, how easy scheduling was, and that the place felt like a new home afterward. The method behind that is simple: walk the space, write a clear quote, then do the prep properly. That means patching, sanding, caulking and priming before the color goes on.

We paint occupied homes and empty apartments between tenants, single rooms and whole houses. For condos and co-ops, we handle the building's certificate of insurance (COI) and work-hour rules (see condo & apartment renovations).

What's included

  • On-site walkthrough and written quote, usually the next day
  • Furniture moving and covering, floor protection and masking
  • Drywall and plaster patching, skim-coating where needed
  • Caulking trim gaps and sanding glossy surfaces
  • Stain-blocking primer over water stains and smoke
  • Two finish coats on walls; ceilings, trim and doors as quoted
  • Kitchen cabinet painting with bonding primer and enamel
  • Daily cleanup and a final walkthrough with you
02S-201

Local conditions

Painting homes and apartments around NYC

Plaster walls. Many Staten Island and Brooklyn homes built before the 1950s have plaster rather than drywall. Plaster cracks behave differently. We cut out, tape and skim them so they don't telegraph back through the new paint.

Apartments and condos. Elevator reservations, building work hours, and COIs naming the condo board and managing agent are routine for us. We'll coordinate directly with your super or management office.

Move-in and turnover painting. Landlords and owners between tenants need a fast, clean turnaround. We schedule around closing dates and lease starts.

FAQ

Interior painting questions

How fast can you give me a painting quote?

After the on-site walkthrough, we usually send a written quote the next day.

Do you provide a COI for my condo or co-op?

Yes. We issue certificates of insurance naming your building, board and managing agent as the building requires, and send them to management before work starts.

Do I need to move my furniture?

No. We move and cover furniture, protect floors, and put everything back. Please put away small valuables and wall art.

What paint do you use?

Premium interior acrylics from major manufacturers. We match sheen to the room: flat or matte on ceilings, eggshell or matte on walls, semi-gloss or satin on trim and doors, and scrubbable finishes in kitchens, baths and kids' rooms.
