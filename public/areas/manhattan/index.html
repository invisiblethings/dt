Manhattan

Area overview

In Manhattan, the hardest part of a renovation is often getting approved to start. Managing agents want a COI with exact wording and limits, boards want an alteration agreement and sometimes drawings, and supers want elevator bookings and quiet hours respected. We deal with all of it as a matter of routine.

We take on Manhattan apartment work of every size: painting before a move-in or sale, new floors, kitchen and bathroom renovations, and full interior refreshes in condos, co-ops and rental units.

What we plan for here

Site conditions

  1. COIs to the building's wording.

    We issue certificates naming the condo or co-op corporation, board and managing agent exactly as the building's sample requires, as soon as we have the requirements.

  2. Alteration agreements.

    Kitchens, baths and anything touching plumbing, electrical or walls typically need board approval. We prepare the contractor's side of the package. See our COI & alteration agreement guide.

  3. Building rules.

    Work hours, freight elevators, corridor protection, debris removal and summer-work rules in some co-ops are all part of our plan from day one.
