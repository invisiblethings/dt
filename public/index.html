Services

Sheet index: what we build

Every service has its own sheet. Pick one for scope, local details and FAQs.

ExteriorRoofs, walls, and everything between the curb and the front door.

  • S-101 Roofing & Roof Replacement Full roof replacements for Staten Island homes, two-families and small commercial buildings, including asphalt shingle, flat roof membrane, flashing and gutters.
  • S-102 Roof Repair & Leak Repair A stain on the ceiling is rarely directly under the leak. We find where the water actually gets in, fix that spot, and show you photos of the repair.
  • S-103 Siding Installation & Replacement New siding over a properly wrapped and flashed wall. Vinyl, insulated vinyl, and fiber-cement, with trim, soffit and fascia to match.
  • S-104 Exterior Painting Most of an exterior paint job is prep, and we do it all. Scrape, sand, repair, prime, caulk, then two finish coats.
  • S-105 Pavers, Patios, Driveways & Masonry Paver driveways, patios and walkways built on an excavated and compacted base, plus stoop, step and retaining-wall masonry.
  • S-106 Decks & Porches New decks, rebuilds and porch repairs on proper footings and properly attached ledgers, in pressure-treated lumber or composite.

InteriorRooms, floors, finishes, and full renovations.

  • S-201 Interior Painting Walls, ceilings, trim and doors for homes, apartments and condos. A quote the day after the walkthrough, and a crew that cleans up like it's their own place.
  • S-202 Kitchen Remodeling Cabinets, counters, tile, floors, lighting and the licensed trades behind them, all on one schedule run by one general contractor.
  • S-203 Bathroom Remodeling Tile showers that are waterproofed behind the tile, tub-to-shower conversions, vanities, ventilation and licensed plumbing.
  • S-204 Basement Finishing & Renovation We fix the moisture first, then insulate, frame and finish with materials that can tolerate a below-grade space.
  • S-205 Flooring Installation Hardwood, engineered wood, luxury vinyl plank and tile, installed over a subfloor we've flattened first.
  • S-206 Whole-Home & Gut Renovations From one floor to the whole house, down to the studs if needed. One general contractor running every trade, with one schedule and one point of contact.
  • S-207 Home Additions & Extensions Rear extensions, second-story additions and dormers, built to approved plans and tied cleanly into the existing roof, siding and interior.

SpecialtyBuildings with boards, managing agents, and business hours.

  • S-301 Condo, Co-op & Apartment Renovations A contractor your managing agent will approve. We provide COIs naming the building, follow the alteration agreement, and work within building hours.
  • S-302 Commercial & Storefront Build-Outs Storefronts, barbershops, lounges, restaurants and offices, built on a schedule that respects your opening date.

FAQ

Questions

What does AXE Contracting Group do?

AXE is a general contractor based on Staten Island. We handle roofing and roof repair, interior and exterior painting, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, flooring, basements, additions, pavers and masonry, decks, siding, and commercial build-outs, plus condo and co-op renovations in Brooklyn and Manhattan.

Where do you work?

All of Staten Island, from St. George to Tottenville, plus Brooklyn and Manhattan.

How do I get an estimate?

Call (347) 265-1229 (Mon–Sat, 8 AM–6 PM) or send the estimate form. We schedule a walkthrough and send a written quote, usually within a day or two of the visit.

Are you insured? Can you provide a COI for my building?

Yes. We're insured and issue certificates of insurance naming your condo or co-op building, board and managing agent as required.

Do you handle electrical and plumbing?

We coordinate licensed NYC electricians and plumbers for any work that requires them, under one contract and one schedule with us.
