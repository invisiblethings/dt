Tom’s music played by others

  • Adam Swanson plays four Brier pieces (“Balderdash,” “Blue Sahara,” “Over the Top” and “Elephant Tracks”) on Revival Ragtime (Rivermont, BSW-2245).
  • Will Perkins performs “Coriander Rag” on Snowy Morning Blues (Rivermont, BSW-2239).
  • Ragtime Wizardry (Vol. 1, 2014; Vol. 2, 2019) is a multi-composer project; Tom’s “Delphinium Rag” was written on commission for it by Danny Matson.

Album details are compiled from tombriermusic.com, Ragtime.nu, Wikipedia and The Syncopated Times. Sources differ slightly on a few years and titles.

Tom Brier

Ragtime pianist and composer from Oakdale, California. More than 200 piano compositions, including “Razor Blades.”
