# On-Page SEO — 72/100

## What works
- Every page has exactly one H1 except one (see finding below)
- Canonical tags present on all pages
- Open Graph title/description/image present on all 45 pages

## Findings

### [Medium] 13 titles exceed 60 characters
13 of 45 pages (29%) have title tags longer than 60 characters and will likely truncate in Google's SERP, e.g. 'Best Free Piano Learning Apps: 7 Apps That Actually Work for Beginners' (80 chars), 'Yamaha P-145 / P-145BT Review in 2026: Still My #1 Pick for Beginners' (69 chars).

**Recommendation:** Trim these titles to under ~60 characters while preserving the primary keyword near the front.

### [Medium] 6 pages missing meta description
cookie-policy, contact, privacy-policy, acoustic-vs-digital-piano, how-to-tune-a-piano-a-simple-guide, and ahmad-jamal-biography have no meta description tag.

**Recommendation:** Write unique 120-160 character descriptions for each, especially the two content pages (acoustic-vs-digital-piano, how-to-tune-a-piano) which are indexable and could otherwise rank.

### [Low] 14 meta descriptions exceed 160 characters
14 pages have descriptions that will truncate in the SERP, up to 224 characters on /pianote-review/.

**Recommendation:** Trim to 150-160 characters, front-loading the value proposition.

### [Medium] Duplicate title and meta description pair
/pianoforall-review/ and /pianoforall-vs-alfreds-piano-book/ share an identical title ("Piano For All Review: A Pro Pianist's Take (2026)") and meta description.

**Recommendation:** Resolve alongside the canonical/duplicate-content issue above — give the comparison page its own unique title and description if kept as a separate page.

### [Low] Two H1s on privacy-policy page
/privacy-policy/ has 2 H1 elements instead of 1.

**Recommendation:** Demote the secondary H1 to an H2, low priority given it's a legal boilerplate page.
