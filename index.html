About the Forum

A stage for composers who live down the street.

Most concert halls program music by composers who died long ago or live far away. The Baltimore Composers' Forum (BCF) exists to do the opposite: find the people writing music in Baltimore right now and get their work in front of an audience.

We're a composer-run, nonprofit presenting organization. We produce concerts and premieres, organize reading sessions so members can hear new scores played by real ensembles, and connect composers with the performers who bring their work to life.

We're funded by member dues, concert donations, gifts from individuals and businesses, and grants, such as a mini-grant from the Maryland State Arts Council.

  1. 1992

    Composer and lutenist Dawn Culbertson founds the Forum.

  2. 1993

    A John Cage memorial concert: But What About the Sound…

  3. 1994

    A concert at the Walters Art Museum alongside a Contemporary Museum exhibit.

  4. 1998

    Reading sessions with a string quartet, and later the Global Percussion Ensemble.

  5. 2004

    Dawn Culbertson dies. The Forum carries on in her memory.

  6. Today

    Concerts at Goucher College, a Baltimore Jazz Composers Showcase at the Eubie Blake Center, and 300+ works presented.

Our founder

Dawn Culbertson (1951–2004)

Composer, lutenist, recorder player, singer, journalist and bassist in an avant-garde big band, Dawn founded the Forum in 1992. A Peabody-trained composer, she spent her career championing music that other people overlooked.

Questions

Good to know

How can I get my music performed in Baltimore?

Join the Forum as a composer member. Members submit works for consideration on BCF concerts, and we've organized reading sessions where ensembles play through members' new scores.

Do I have to live in Baltimore to join?

The Forum is for composers based in Baltimore and the surrounding area. Performers and listeners are welcome wherever they live.

How much does membership cost?

$50 a year, or $25 for students. Members also get discounted admission to ticketed BCF events.

Are donations tax-deductible?

Yes. The BCF is a registered nonprofit, and contributions are tax-deductible to the extent the law allows.

How do I find out about upcoming concerts?

Join the free events mailing list. We email about two weeks before each event.
