# About Pianoers

Pianoers is an independent website about learning and playing the piano. We review pianos, keyboards, apps and online courses, and we write the guides we wish we'd had when we started: how to practise, how to look after an instrument, and how to choose lessons that suit you.

## Who we write for

- Adults starting the piano for the first time, or returning after a long break
- Parents choosing a first instrument or lessons for a child
- Beginners who want to avoid buying the wrong keyboard or course
- Players ready to upgrade and looking for a fair comparison

## How we review

Every review and guide on Pianoers follows the same rules.

- **We check the facts at the source.** Prices, specifications and refund terms come from the manufacturer's or course provider's own pages, checked at the time of writing. Each review shows when it was last checked.
- **We say how we know what we know.** When we've used a product ourselves, the review says so. When a section draws on manufacturer data, independent reviews or reporting, we name the source.
- **We cover the downsides.** Every review says who a product suits and who should skip it. If a cheaper option does the same job, we tell you.
- **We stick to evidence.** We'll say so when a course costs too much or a product falls short, and we back that up. We don't make claims about people or companies we can't support.
- **We keep reviews up to date.** Courses change their prices, apps add features and keyboards are discontinued. We revisit our reviews and update them when the facts change.

## How we make money

Pianoers is free to read. Some links on the site are affiliate links: if you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We mark these in our reviews.

Affiliate commissions never decide what we recommend or how we score a product. When a free or cheaper option does the job, we recommend it, even if a pricier one would earn us more. Companies can't pay for a review, a ranking or a better score.

## Corrections

We work hard to get things right, and when we don't, we fix it. If you spot an error, an out-of-date price or a broken link, [contact us](https://pianoers.com/contact/) and we'll correct it. Significant changes to a review are noted on the page.
