# Before you publish

1. **The "one of the biggest" sentence.** It sits at the top in bold brackets,
   with an HTML comment above it. Fill in your real monthly readers from Google
   Analytics or Search Console and remove the brackets, or delete the paragraph.
   The site has 38 posts and two authors; if the number doesn't clearly support
   "one of the biggest", leave it out. An About page that overclaims loses the
   trust it's there to build, and in some countries (the UK and EU among them)
   comparative size claims need evidence behind them.

2. **"How we review" is a promise.** It says reviews state when something was
   tested hands-on and when they're based on research. The Loog, Skoove and Ridley
   rewrites already follow that. Older reviews that claim hands-on testing should
   either be backed up or reworded, or this section won't hold.

3. **Affiliate links need marking.** The page says affiliate links are marked in
   reviews. Add a one-line disclosure near the top of any post with affiliate
   links (for example the Pianoforall links at pianoers.com/PFA), such as:
   "Some links in this review are affiliate links. If you buy through them we may
   earn a commission, at no extra cost to you." The US FTC expects this to be
   clear and close to the links, not only on the About page.

4. **Check the facts in the bios:**
   - Founded in 2021 (from the current About page)
   - Richard: playing since age 8, gigs, has taught hundreds of students (from his
     author bio)
   - Katarina: teaching since 2001, B.A. Music, University of Missouri, MTNA
     member since 1995. The current About page names the organisation as
     "National Association of Music Teachers"; her author bio now says MTNA, so
     I've used MTNA. Confirm 1995 is right.

# What changed from the current page

- Replaced the "no-BS", "garbage", "sucks" tone with plain, confident language.
  Personality reads as fun on a blog post; on the page people check before
  trusting a review, calm reads as credible.
- Removed "If something is a scam, we call it". That stance is what led to the
  old Stephen Ridley page. Replaced with "We stick to evidence".
- Removed "We buy the gear, we take the courses, we sit through every lesson".
  It's a strong claim that every review would have to live up to. "How we
  review" says what you can stand behind.
- Added sections the old page lacked and that Google's quality guidelines and AI
  answer engines look for: credentials, how reviews are made, how the site makes
  money, a corrections policy and a contact route.
- Added links to four starter guides, so the About page sends visitors on into
  the site.
- Added Organization and Person schema so search engines and AI assistants can
  connect the site to its authors and their credentials.
