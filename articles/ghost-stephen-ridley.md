Stephen Ridley's Complete Piano Masterclass is an online piano course sold by Ridley Academy, the company the British pianist founded around 2019. It is built around a chord-based approach to playing pop songs, runs to more than 200 video lessons, and lists at $2,997, though it is most often offered through a free webinar at between $1,397 and $1,997.

The course teaches a real and useful skill: playing popular songs from chords. The same skill is taught by courses that cost a fraction of the price, and the refund terms have been the main source of buyer complaints. If you are considering it, read the price comparison and the refund section before you buy.

## Who is Stephen Ridley?

Stephen Ridley is a British pianist from the north of England who worked in investment banking in London before leaving to perform full time. A 2012 post about quitting his banking job to become a musician was picked up by Business Insider and Bloomberg, according to WBUR. He then built an audience playing piano in public in London and online.

He founded Ridley Academy around 2019. The company says it has more than 40,000 students in over 60 countries; that figure comes from the company, and I found no outside source for it. Ridley's promotional material describes performances for large audiences and private clients, which are likewise his own claims.

His public profile is that of a performer and entertainer rather than a conservatory-trained teacher, and the course reflects that: it focuses on playing songs in a confident, expressive style rather than on formal technique or reading.

## What's in the course

The official course page describes two video classes delivered together:

- **The Complete Piano Masterclass**, the core course, covering Ridley's chord-based method for playing songs
- **The Scales Masterclass**, covering scales and finger exercises

Ridley Academy presents these as a 6 to 12 month programme of more than 200 lessons. The checkout page also lists supporting material, including an exercise pack, a music dictionary, and a guide to choosing a piano or keyboard. Access does not expire, and the lessons play in a browser.

Ridley Academy also sells add-ons and bundles, including the Artist Development Bootcamp, a four-day in-person retreat with about 40 hours of coaching. These cost extra, on top of the Masterclass.

## The Ridley Method: four chords and pop songs

The course's central idea is that a beginner can play a large number of popular songs by learning a small set of chords and a few ways to play them. Ridley's ads show the I–V–vi–IV progression, which in C major is C, G, A minor and F.

The method itself is sound. That progression sits under a remarkable number of pop songs, a fact the comedy band The Axis of Awesome made famous with their "[Four Chord Song](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5pidokakU4I)." Many piano teachers introduce it in the first lessons for the same reason Ridley does: it gets a beginner playing recognisable music within days, and that keeps them practising.

![Stephen Ridley teaching four chords in one of his YouTube videos](https://pianoers.com/content/images/2023/12/Instantly-Play-100-Songs-By-Learning-These-4-Chords---Piano-1-30-screenshot.webp)

The gaps matter as much as the strengths. Chord-based playing doesn't teach you to read sheet music, play classical pieces, or develop the finger independence that harder repertoire needs. It's one well-established route into the piano, not a replacement for the others.

The marketing claims deserve a realistic reading. Ridley's site has promised "up to 40 new songs in 1 hour," and his ads describe the programme as taking "six to twelve months of three minutes per day." Truth in Advertising, a consumer watchdog, pointed out that such results are not what most buyers should expect, and noted that it took Ridley around 20 years to reach his own level. A realistic expectation for an adult beginner is to play simple chord accompaniments to many songs within a few months of regular practice, which is a worthwhile result.

## How the course is sold

Most buyers come to the Masterclass through a free online webinar, often promoted in YouTube and social media ads. Journalists from WBUR and Truth in Advertising who attended these webinars in 2024 described them as long presentations ending in a sales offer.

Points both outlets documented:

- The webinar is free, and the course offered at the end is not.
- The offer price varied between attendees, from about $1,400 to $1,997, presented as a discount from $2,997.
- Truth in Advertising reported that a "60 minute" offer was still available four days later by email, with financing that hadn't been mentioned in the webinar.

None of this changes what the course contains, but it's useful to know before you join a webinar. You don't need to decide during it, and the price on the checkout page is the one that counts.

## Refund terms: read them before you buy

Check this area first, because the published terms and buyers' experiences don't line up.

- **The official course page** says: "We offer full refunds up to 28 days after purchase."
- **Webinar offers** have described a "lifetime failproof guarantee." Truth in Advertising reported that this guarantee required buyers to finish the course before qualifying for a refund, and that some customers said this prevented them from getting their money back.
- **The Better Business Bureau** lists Ridley Academy as not BBB accredited, with an F rating and 16 complaints closed in the last three years, as of October 2026.

If you buy, get the refund conditions that apply to your order in writing, note the date 28 days after purchase, and decide before then whether the course is working for you. Keep your receipt and any correspondence.

## How the price compares

The list price of $2,997 is far above most online piano courses, and even the typical offer price of $1,397 sits well above the market.

For perspective, Skoove's annual plan costs €149.99 a year, so the Masterclass's list price would pay for about 20 years of Skoove, and the $1,397 offer for about 9 years. Courses that teach the same chord-based approach, such as Pianoforall, are sold as a one-time purchase at a much lower price. A year of weekly private lessons with a local teacher can also cost less than the Masterclass list price in many areas, with the advantage of someone watching your hands.

The extra money buys Ridley's personal style and presentation. Some students find that motivating; whether it's worth the difference is a judgment only you can make.

## Who the course suits

**It may suit you if:**

- You're an adult beginner whose goal is playing pop songs from chords
- Ridley's style and personality motivate you to practise
- The price is comfortable for you, and you've confirmed the refund terms

**Look elsewhere if:**

- You want to read music or play classical repertoire
- You want feedback on your playing as you go
- You want the most learning for your money
- You're buying for a child

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Stephen Ridley's Piano Masterclass cost?

The list price is $2,997. In practice it's most often offered through a free webinar at between $1,397 and $1,997, presented as a discount, and a 12-month payment plan is available. Add-ons such as the in-person Artist Development Bootcamp cost extra.

### Is Ridley Academy legit?

Ridley Academy is a real company selling a real course of more than 200 video lessons. Criticism of it has centred on the price, the webinar sales approach, and refund conditions rather than on whether the lessons exist. Check the refund terms for your order before buying.

### What is the Ridley Method?

It's Stephen Ridley's chord-based approach to playing popular songs. It centres on the I–V–vi–IV progression (C, G, A minor and F in C major), which appears in many pop songs, together with ways of playing those chords with both hands. It's a well-established way to start, also taught by many teachers and cheaper courses.

### Does Ridley Academy offer refunds?

The official course page offers full refunds up to 28 days after purchase. Some webinar offers have described a "lifetime" guarantee that, according to Truth in Advertising, required finishing the course first. Get the conditions for your order in writing and decide within 28 days.

### Who is Stephen Ridley?

A British pianist from the north of England who left a career in investment banking around 2012 to perform, built an audience playing in public in London and online, and founded Ridley Academy around 2019.

### Can you learn 40 songs in an hour with Ridley's method?

Not as a typical result. Learning four chords lets you play simple accompaniments to many songs, but playing them well takes regular practice over weeks and months. Truth in Advertising noted that headline results like this aren't what most buyers should expect.

### Is the Piano Masterclass good for beginners?

It's aimed at beginners and teaches a useful chord-based skill. It doesn't teach sheet music reading or classical technique, and it doesn't give feedback on your playing. Beginners can learn the same approach from cheaper courses.

### Is the Ridley Academy course worth it?

That depends on how much you value Ridley's personal teaching style. The content covers a well-known approach that cheaper courses also teach, so most learners will get more for their money elsewhere. If you do buy, confirm the refund terms first.

## The verdict

Stephen Ridley's Piano Masterclass teaches a legitimate, practical way to start playing the piano: chords first, songs soon after, confidence early. Ridley is an engaging presenter, and some learners find that the thing that keeps them at the keyboard.

Its weaknesses are the price, which is several times what comparable courses charge, and refund terms that buyers have found harder to use than the marketing suggests. Neither is hidden if you know where to look.

If the price is comfortable and his style motivates you, the course will teach you to play songs from chords. If you want the same skill for less, or want to read music, start with one of the alternatives above.
