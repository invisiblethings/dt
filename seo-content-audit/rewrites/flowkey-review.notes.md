## The big change: Flowkey restructured its plans

The original page described one plan sold monthly ($19.99), for six months ($83.94 / $83.99) or yearly ($119.88 / $119.99), and the IMAGE-5 screenshot (January 2025) shows the same. Flowkey's help center now (article dated 4 August 2026) lists **three tiers (Classic, Premium, Family), monthly or yearly only, no 6-month plan, no lifetime plan**. Classic covers courses plus classical and public-domain songs; pop, rock, jazz and film music need Premium. The rewrite uses the current structure throughout.
