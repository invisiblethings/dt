Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my roof needs repair or full replacement?

If your roof is leaking, missing shingles, or over 20 years old, it may be time for replacement. Our team will inspect it honestly and recommend whether a cost-effective repair is enough or if a full roof replacement is the smarter investment.

How long does a roof installation or replacement take?

Most residential projects are completed in 1–2 days, depending on size and weather. Larger commercial or flat roofing systems may take longer, but we always work efficiently to minimize disruption.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We provide fast, no-obligation estimates so you can make an informed decision. Transparency is important to us, no hidden fees, no surprises.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. B&B Roofing is fully licensed and insured in both New York and New Jersey. You’re protected from start to finish when you choose us.

What types of roofing do you work on?

We handle asphalt shingles, flat roofing (TPO, EPDM), commercial systems, and specialty projects like skylights, gutters, and siding. Whether it’s a family home, brownstone, or commercial property, we have you covered.

Do you offer warranties on your work?

Yes. Our installations come with manufacturer warranties on materials plus our own workmanship guarantee, giving you long-term peace of mind.

Can you help with emergency roofing services?

Yes. Storm damage doesn’t wait, and neither do we. Our emergency response team is ready to handle leaks, fallen shingles, and urgent repairs quickly.

Why should I choose B&B Roofing over other companies?

Because we combine local experience, premium materials, and honest service. We don’t cut corners, we show up when we say we will, and we treat your property with respect. That’s why so much of our business comes from referrals.
