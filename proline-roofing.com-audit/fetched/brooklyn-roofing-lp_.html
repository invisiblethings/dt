Brooklyn Roof Problems We Fix

Brooklyn's Most Common Roof Problems, Solved

From leaky flat roofs and failing parapet walls to storm-damaged shingles and chimney flashing, we know exactly why Brooklyn homes leak, and how to stop the damage for good.

Flat Roof Leaks & Ponding Water

The #1 Brooklyn roof problem. After 15 to 20 years, rowhouse and brownstone membranes start to blister, crack, and pool water. We find the true source of the leak (rarely where the stain is) and patch, resurface, or recoat.
Same-day inspection • TPO, EPDM, rubber • All rowhouse types

Free Leak Inspection

Parapet Wall & Coping Repair

The hidden source of most rowhouse leaks. Cracked brick, failing mortar, and missing coping stones on shared parapet walls let water travel straight into your top floor. We repoint, reflash, and rebuild parapets to stop water at the source.
Repointing • Coping replacement • Full waterproofing

Free Parapet Assessment

Chimney Flashing & Repair

Brownstone chimneys are famous for leaks. Old flashing lifts, brick crumbles, and water runs straight down interior walls. We reflash, repoint, and cap chimneys to keep them dry and structurally sound for decades.
New flashing • Brick repointing • Chimney caps

Free Chimney Check

Skylight & Bulkhead Leaks

Every Brooklyn rowhouse has one, and both leak. Roof hatches and skylights are the two most common failure points on a flat roof. We reseal, reflash, and replace failed units with leak-proof, energy-efficient installs.
New units • Leak-proof flashing • All sizes

Free Skylight Quote

Roof Drains & Gutter Backup

Clogged drains equal ponding water equal leaks. Internal roof drains, scuppers, and rear-yard gutters clog fast on tight Brooklyn lots. We clear, repair, and upgrade the whole drainage system so water actually leaves your roof.
Drain clearing • Scupper repair • Seamless gutters

Free Drainage Estimate

Asphalt Shingle Repair & Replacement

Storm damage, missing shingles, or an aging roof? From Marine Park to Dyker Heights, Brooklyn's single-family and semi-detached homes take a real beating from nor'easters. We repair damaged sections or install new architectural shingles built to last decades.
Architectural shingles • Storm damage • Insurance claim help

Free Shingle Estimate

Full Flat Roof Replacement

When patching stops working, replace it once and be done. We install TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen systems built for NYC weather, backed by up to 50-year material warranties and a full workmanship guarantee.
TPO, EPDM, rubber • Workmanship warranty • Upfront pricing

Free Replacement Quote

Your Brooklyn Roof Questions, Answered

Straight answers to the 8 things Brooklyn homeowners ask us most.

Do you offer free roof estimates in Brooklyn? +

Yes. 100% free, no obligation. A licensed Brooklyn roofing expert comes to your home, evaluates every leak point (including hard-to-reach parapets, chimneys, and flat-roof drains), and gives you an upfront written quote.

How much does a new roof cost in Brooklyn? MOST ASKED +

Flat roof replacements typically run $8,000 to $25,000+, depending on size, material (TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen), and access. Shingle roofs vary by pitch and complexity. Every Brooklyn building is different, so every quote is custom.

Get your custom quote →

Do you handle emergency roof leaks? +

Yes. 24/7 emergency response across Brooklyn. If your roof is leaking right now, call us. We tarp fast to stop the damage, then come back for the permanent fix.

Call (347) 323-9128 now →

What roof works best for Brooklyn rowhouses and brownstones? +

For flat rowhouse and brownstone roofs, TPO, EPDM rubber, or modified bitumen offer the best balance of durability, waterproofing, and cost. For pitched shingle roofs (Marine Park, Mill Basin, Dyker Heights), architectural asphalt shingles are the sweet spot.

We recommend the right system during your free estimate.

How do I know if I need a new roof? +

Watch for these warning signs:

  • Roof is 15 to 20 years old or more
  • Ponding water or blisters on your flat roof
  • Missing, curling, or cracked shingles
  • Water stains on ceilings or top-floor walls
  • Sagging roofline or rising energy bills

Spot any of these? Book a free estimate before small issues become big ones →

How long does a Brooklyn roof job take? +

Most rowhouse flat roofs and shingle replacements take 1 to 2 days. Larger buildings or complex jobs (chimney work, parapet rebuilds, structural repairs) can take 3 to 5 days. Your quote includes a detailed timeline so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you help with insurance claims? +

Yes. Storm damage, leaks, and structural roof issues are often covered by homeowners insurance. We document everything with detailed photos and reports, and work directly with your adjuster to help get your claim approved. No extra cost.

What warranties do you offer? +

Up to 50 years on materials (depending on the product) plus a solid workmanship guarantee on labor. Every job comes with warranty paperwork in writing, so you're covered for the long haul.
