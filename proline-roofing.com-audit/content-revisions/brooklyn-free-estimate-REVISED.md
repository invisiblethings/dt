## What's Included in Your Free Roofing Estimate

A real roofing estimate is more than a number on a napkin. Every free estimate from ProLine Roofing includes:

- A full roof inspection by a local, licensed roofer — not a subcontractor or a sales rep
- Precise roof square footage calculation
- A clear breakdown of repair vs. replacement costs, so you can compare both options
- Itemized pricing for materials (shingles, flashing, underlayment, and more)
- Options across roofing types — flat roofs, asphalt shingles, metal, and more
- An estimated labor timeline, so you know what to expect before work starts
- An easy-to-read quote with no fine print

We don't throw out a number over the phone. A real person inspects your roof and explains your options — whether that's a small patch, a section repair, or a full roof replacement.

## Free Roof Estimate vs. Roofing Quote: Is There a Difference?

Not really — "roof estimate" and "roofing quote" describe the same thing, and we use both terms because that's how people actually search for this. What matters is what's behind the number: a rough phone estimate and a real on-site roofing quote can differ by thousands of dollars once someone's actually looked at your roof. Every quote we give is based on an in-person inspection, so the number you get is the number you can plan around.

## Residential and Commercial Free Roof Estimates

We provide free roof estimates for both homeowners and property managers across Brooklyn:

- **Residential:** Single-family homes, brownstones, and multi-family buildings — repair, replacement, and re-roofing estimates.
- **Commercial:** Flat roof and low-slope roofing estimates for commercial properties, including maintenance contracts and larger multi-unit buildings.

## Free Supplemental Roofing Estimates for Insurance Claims

If your insurance adjuster's initial estimate came in lower than what the repair or replacement actually costs, you may be entitled to a supplemental claim. We provide free supplemental roofing estimates that document the difference — itemized and detailed enough to submit directly to your insurance company. This is common after storm damage, when an adjuster's first pass misses code-required upgrades, underlayment, or flashing work.

## Bonus: What Affects Your Roofing Cost?

A handful of factors move the price up or down more than anything else:

- Roof size and shape
- Type of shingles or materials
- Number of existing layers that need to be removed
- Any structural repairs needed underneath
- Labor and roof accessibility
- Local permit requirements

Don't guess — get a real number. That's what your free estimate is for.

**What's included in a free roof estimate?**
A full in-person inspection, precise square footage measurement, a repair-vs-replacement cost breakdown, itemized material pricing, and an estimated labor timeline — all with no obligation to book any work.

**Do you offer free estimates for commercial roofing in Brooklyn?**
